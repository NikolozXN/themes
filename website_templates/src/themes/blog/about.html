Hello, I'm Elena.

Writer, photographer, and storyteller passionate about intentional living and mindful moments.

My Story

I am a writer, photographer, and storyteller based in the Pacific Northwest. I started The Daily Journal as a way to document my journey towards a more intentional life.

Over the years, this space has evolved into a community of like-minded individuals who appreciate the beauty in the ordinary, the value of slowing down, and the power of thoughtful storytelling.

What began as a personal exploration has become a platform for sharing ideas, experiences, and inspiration with readers around the world.

What I Do

When I'm not writing, you can find me hiking in the mountains, brewing coffee, or reading a good book by the fire. These moments of quiet reflection fuel my creativity and inform my work.

I believe in the power of slow living, mindful consumption, and finding beauty in everyday moments. My writing explores themes of intentionality, simplicity, and the art of living well.

Through photography and words, I aim to capture the essence of moments that might otherwise go unnoticed—the morning light through a window, the perfect cup of coffee, the quiet satisfaction of a well-lived day.

What I Believe

The principles that guide my work and life

Intentional Living

Making conscious choices about how we spend our time, energy, and resources. Living with purpose and presence.

Authentic Connection

Building genuine relationships with readers, nature, and ourselves. Finding meaning in shared experiences and stories.

Mindful Moments

Slowing down to appreciate the beauty around us. Finding joy in simple pleasures and everyday experiences.

Let's Connect

I'm always open to new collaborations, conversations, and connections. Whether you're interested in working together or just want to say hello, I'd love to hear from you.
